1. Connect to Seerr

JellyStream uses your Seerr (Jellyseerr / Overseerr) to see what's on each service and to send requests. The API key stays on your server.

2. Services

Shown in this order. Turn services on or off, rename them, or add any provider Seerr knows about. Open a service to fine-tune which plans and stores count.

Rent/buy titles go to the service tied to that store (for example Amazon Video → Prime Video, Apple TV store → Apple TV+). Set these stores per service.

3. Home screen

Adds a small "JellyStream diagnostics" button to every page. It records page changes and errors so a problem can be copied or sent to the server log. Turn it off when you're done.

4. Service pages

Trending uses TMDB's daily/weekly trending list, filtered to each service. If a service has fewer trending titles than the list length, popular titles fill the rest.
Netflix's own weekly Top 10 for your region, published each Tuesday. Falls back to the setting above if it can't be loaded. Other services don't publish theirs.

5. Intros

Give a service an intro video and it plays, with sound, when you switch to that service (Jellyfin's plays when you switch back to Jellyfin). Phones use their own (portrait) video; a phone without one gets the built-in animation. It plays full screen with no player controls. Services without one show the built-in animation. Short clips (a few seconds of 720p or 1080p H.264 MP4) load instantly; they're kept by the browser after the first play and preloaded on the home screen.
